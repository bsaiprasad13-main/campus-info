IAR

1.1
Vision
The
International
&
Alumni
Relations
sphere
serves
as
the
prominent
representative
of
IIT
Madras
within
and
beyond
the
institution.
Its
primary
objective
is
to
enhance
alumni
relations,
cultivate
a
favourable
local
and
global
perception
of
IIT
Madras,
foster
valuable
corporate
opportunities,
and
actively
promote
and
facilitate
international
exchanges.
The
IAR
sphere
encompasses
esteemed
entities
such
as
the
Office
of
Alumni
&
Corporate
Relations,
the
Office
of
Global
Engagement,
the
IIT
Madras
Alumni
Association,
the
Heritage
Centre
and
the
IAR
Student
Council.
Through
cohesive
collaboration,
these
entities
diligently
cooperate
with
other
student
and
administrative
organizations,
strategically
aligning
efforts
to
achieve
the
visionary
goals
envisioned
by
IITM.

2.2.1
Chennai36
Vision
Chennai
36,
or
C36
as
it
is
fondly
called,
is
the
writing
arm
of
IAR
Council's
Branding
Cell,
the
official
student
and
alumni
blog
within
the
institute.
We
aim
to
connect
the
various
stakeholders
groups
like
current
students,
alumni,
etc
through
the
medium
of
writing.
The
main
goal
of
Chennai36
is
to
highlight
the
accomplishments,
experiences,
and
stories
of
IIT
Madras
students
and
alumni,
while
engaging
with
the
faculty
and
fostering
dialogues
for
showcasing
the
research
and
innovation
in
the
institute.
The
blog
focuses
on
a
variety
of
topics,
such
as
research
endeavours,
entrepreneurial
ventures,
extracurricular
activities,
and
interviews
with
eminent
alumni.
It
provides
an
avenue
for
recognising
the
successes
and
efforts
of
the
IIT
Madras
community
while
also
educating
and
motivating
its
audience.
Role
of
the
team
in
the
IAR
Student
Council
C36
conducts
interviews
and
writes
articles
about
various
topics
ranging
from
internships
to
general
fundaes.
We
also
conduct
the
DAA
interviews
and
are
involved
in
producing
the
content
for
the
yearbook.
This
year,
C36,
apart
from
its
regular
activities,
will
also
be
managing/producing
content
for
Campus
Times,
a
campus
reprise
of
the
older
Campastimes.
Some
of
our
regular
interactions
and
collaborations
include:
●
DAA
Interviews:
IITMAA,
Alumni
Relations
Office
Yearbook:
ARC,
Design
Team
●
Campus
Times:
Heritage
Club,
Design
Team
●
Blog/Website:
WebOps,
Design
Team
