SELECTION

The

ex-reps

●
The

well-drafted
written
application

interview

the
core(s)

●
All
applications
will
be

+

2.1.13
REMOVAL
PROCEDURE:
●
Unsatisfactory
Performance:
●
If
a
core
is
found
performing
way
below
expectations,
he/she
can
be
removed
from
the
SOC
following
due
procedure.
An
initial
warning
shall
be
given,
with
a
notice
period
of
two
weeks.
If
the
performance
does
not
improve
in
two
weeks,
the
Head(s)
of
the
SOC,
in
consultation
with
ISS
and
Advisor
(Sports),
can
choose
to
remove
a
core
from
the
team.
A
report
on
the
same
shall
be
submitted
to
Advisor
(Sports)
and
shall
be
made
available
to
the
SLC.
●
If
a
coordinator
is
found
performing
way
below
expectations,
the
concerned
core
shall
issue
a
warning,
with
the
Head(s)
of
the
SOC
in
the
loop.
If
the
performance
does
not
improve
in
two
weeks,
the
coordinator
shall
be
removed
from
the
team,
in
consultation
with
the
Head(s)
of
the
SOC,
ISS,
and
Advisor
(Sports).
A
report
on
the
same
shall
be
prepared
by
the
concerned
core
and
presented
to
the
ISS.
●
Financial
Misappropriation:
●
When
a
complaint
is

Sports
Action
Committee,

(FAC).
The

(Sports).
The
