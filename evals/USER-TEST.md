# The user test: does it help a real junior?

The scripts check the app against our own questions. They can't tell us what matters most: whether a first- or
second-year finds what they need, learns about something they didn't know existed, and would come back. Only real
students can tell us that.

**Status:** written on 26 Nov 2026, not run yet.

## Who, and how

- 5 first- or second-years from different departments who haven't used Campus Info. Nobody from the editing team.
- 10 minutes each, in person or on a video call with their screen shared, on their own phone.
- No help and no hints. If someone is stuck for a minute, write it down and move on.

## 1. Before (1 minute)

"Name every club, team, competition, scholarship or programme you know you could join or apply for this year."
Write down how many they name.

## 2. Five tasks (5 minutes)

For each task, write down: found it (yes / only with a hint / no), roughly how long it took, and whether they
searched or browsed.

1. "You like building things. Find a team or club you could join, what you'd get from it, and how to get in."
2. "Find a scholarship you might qualify for, and what it gives you."
3. "You'd like to do research with a professor next summer. Find out how to start."
4. "It's 2 a.m. and your friend has fainted in the hostel. Get help." Stop them before they call.
5. "Find out how and when you can change your mess."

## 3. After (3 minutes)

- "Did you find anything here you didn't know existed? Would you try it?" Count the new things, and how many they'd
  act on.
- Four quick ratings, from 1 to 5: "I found what I was looking for." "I trust what it says." "I'd open it again."
  "I'd send it to a junior."
- "What did you look for that wasn't there?"

## What counts as passing

Set before the first session, so the results can't move the bar:

- 4 of the 5 students finish at least 4 of the 5 tasks without a hint.
- Every student finds at least one thing they didn't know about and would consider.
- In the 2 a.m. task, every student reaches a Call button within 30 seconds.
- The middle score for "I'd open it again" is 4 or more.

Every failed task and every "wasn't there" becomes a fix, a new page for the seniors to write, or a new question in
the search checks.
