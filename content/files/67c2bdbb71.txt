Roles and Responsibilities 
 
The Students' General Secretary shall: 
• Coordinate the work of the other Executive Council members; 
• Represent the views of the General Student Body to the Senate; 
• Handle matters and monitor organizations, committees or clubs which do not fall under the 
purview of other Executive Council members; 
• Represent the students in all tendering processes for student facilities inside the Institute; 
• Be an ex-officio member of any committee which may be set up by the Administration for 
matters related to the general amenities provided to the students; 
• Be one of the student members of the hospital, transport, canteen, security and other such 
committees which may be formed for providing amenities to the students; 
• Be a member of the Hostel Disciplinary Committee (HDC), the Committee for Monitoring of 
General Facilities for Students (CMGFS), the Mess Monitoring and Control Committee 
(MMCC) 
• Be an ex-officio member of any committee/team/organization to represent students in 
matters related to disciplinary action. 
 
Student Bodies fall under the Students’ General Secretary

SGS EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE 
 
SGS Executive Committee monitors the issues affecting the students. It resolves 
them as the earliest, devises initiatives to improve the student community's lives, 
and coordinates and assists various SGS Bodies in their functioning. 
 
Initiatives: 
Before taking any steps forward in implementing any initiatives, the initiative 
mission-vision statement explaining the goals of the initiatives should be sent to 
the SGS for consultation and approval.  
 
Structure: 
The Team Structure is: 
• Core(only 1) 
• Member 
 
Core Selection: 
Core Selection is initiated and processed by a Panel containing the Students’ 
General Secretary, former Students’ General Secretary, and former SGS Executive 
Committee Core. The application procedure will be intimated to the student 
community in a call for applications and they should have CGPA greater than or 
equal to 6.5. 
 
Member: 
Selection is done by the Core and Students’ General Secretary through a process 
intimated to the student community in a call for application forms

CAMPUS FACILITIES MONITORING 
COMMITTEE 
 
The Campus facilities monitoring committee is an initiative for monitoring various 
facilities like lights, security systems, basic civil infrastructure, 
transportation(shuttle service) and other things that don't come under any specific 
body.  
 
ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:  
• To inspect, supervise, and manage facilities that do not fall under the 
jurisdiction of a certain entity or secretary.  
• To examine any facility flaws based on student feedback and resolve the issue. 
TEAM STRUCTURE:  
• Core(Maximum of 3) 
• Coordinators 
CORE SELECTIONS 
Core Selection is initiated and processed by a Panel containing Professors-In 
Charge (DoSt, Faculty advisors and invitees), Students’ General Secretary, and 
former CFMC Cores. The application procedure will be intimated to the student 
community in a call for applications and they should have CGPA greater than or 
equal to 6.5.  
 
COORDINATOR SELECTIONS  
Selection is done by cores and SGS through a process intimated to the student 
community in a call for applications.
