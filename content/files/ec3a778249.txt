secretary and all departments work together throughout the year to conduct a 4 -day Technical Festival

Rule Book for Centre for Innovation, IIT Madras 
Centre for Innovation (CFI) is a ‘Student Lab’ at IIT Madras. Set up with funds donated by the batch of  
1981, it is set in the building that houses the Central Workshop. CFI was started in 2008 with an objective 
to be a forum for creative output of the budding engineers of IIT Madras. It provides students the  
necessary platform for realizing their ideas. 
 
 
Organizational Structure 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Please note that the members of the Advisory Committee don’t belong to the student body. 
 
 
Advisory Committee 
This committee comprises of Dean (Students), Faculty Advisors of CFI and other faculties and professionals 
of stakeholder organizations that have an independent say for the prosperity of CFI. 
 
Roles and responsibilities 
• To synergize initiatives and develop strong foundations for association between CFI and respective 
organization 
• Point out redundancies in existing models, suggest resources and provide newer opportunities CFI to 
explore 
 
Student Advisors 
This committee comprises students of IIT M who were CFI Club Heads / Cores in the past and act as a 
mentor to help clubs with the projects, help in knowledge transfer and provide a matured understanding. 
 
Roles and responsibilities 
• Bringing an independent perspective to the current club’s deliberations and decisions 
 
Co-Curricular Affairs Secretary 
The CoCAS is the elected executive wing member responsible to advise and represent the interest of CFI 
in the students’ legislative council. 
 
Roles and Responsibilities 
• Advisory position, parallel to CFI head, shall be accountable for the functioning of CFI along with CFI 
Head 
• Facilitate effective communication channel between other teams of co-curricular sphere and CFI

Eligibility and Selection Processes 
The eligibility criteria for the various positions in CFI are as below. These add on to the eligibility criteria  
for PoRs in the Students’ Constitution of IIT Madras. These are subject to change under the discretion of  
the Faculty Advisor, Co-Curricular Affairs Secretary, CFI Head and the various core team members. 
 
 
CFI Head: 
 
Eligibility: No specific criteria 
Selection Process: 
• Application for the post shall be circulated via smail by the outgoing CFI Head 
• All applicants shall undergo an interview 
• The selection panel shall include CoCAS, the outgoing CFI head and the CFI faculty advisor 
Core Team Positions: 
Eligibility: No Specific Criteria 
Selection Process: 
• Application for the post shall be circulated via smail by the CFI Head 
• All applicants shall undergo an interview 
• The selection panel shall include CFI Head, CFI faculty advisor and the respective core(s) of the 
preceding year 
 
Coordinators and other positions: 
 
Eligibility: No specific criteria 
Selection Process: 
• Applications for the position shall be circulated via smail by the respective member of the core team 
under whom the position lies 
• All applicants shall undergo an interview 
• The selection panel shall include the respective core team members

Rule Book for Entrepreneurship Cell, IIT Madras 
Entrepreneurship Cell IIT Madras makes the entrepreneurial world accessible to all group of people part  
of the IIT Madras ecosystem, cater the audience comprising from startup enthusiasts to full-time 
entrepreneurs through plethora of yearlong events like Conclaves, Keynotes, Industry Defined Problems, 
B-Planning and fundraising competitions and workshops. 
 
E-Summit is an ISO-Certified flagship event conducted by E-Cell IIT Madras to foster entrepreneurship by 
bringing in entrepreneurial enthusiasts, startup founders, mentors and investors. 
 
 
Organizational Structure 
 
 
Please note that the members of the Steering Committee and Chairperson Committee don’t belong to the 
student body. 
 
Steering Committee 
This committee comprises of Dean (Students), Faculty Advisors of E-Cell and other faculties and 
professionals of stakeholder organizations that have an independent say for the prosperity of 
entrepreneurship ecosystem of the institute. 
 
Roles and responsibilities 
• To synergize initiatives and develop strong foundations for association between E-Cell and respective 
organization 
• Point out redundancies in existing models, suggest resources and provide newer opportunities for E- 
Cell to explore

Rule Book for Nirmaan, IIT Madras 
Nirmaan is the “Pre-Incubation Cell” of IIT Madras, aiming to provide the experience of entrepreneurship 
to the institute community. Inaugurated on 31st October 2015 as a co-curricular sphere initiative, Nirmaan 
is a sandbox for students to practice the highs and lows of entrepreneurship encouraging them to think  
beyond placements. It makes it possible by providing the students with workspace, seed funding, 
mentorship and numerous opportunities to network with alumni and entrepreneurs.

Rule Book for Institute Technical Society, IIT Madras 
Institute Technical Society (TechSoc) synergizes the events of all the organizations of Co-Curricular Sphere 
and caters to the entire student community of IIT Madras through events that ensure overall exposure to 
domains concerning Co -Curricular affairs. The yearlong Inter Hostel Competition consisting of tech -
related competitions and workshops for the same are conducted by the TechSoc Events team and two 
10-week long product building sprints are conducted as a part of BuildSchool.  
 
Inter-IIT Tech Meet is the annual technical competition among all the participating IITs and some of the  
TechSoc IHC events act as a formal selection procedure for students to participate in the Inter -IIT Tech 
Meet.
