 Section I: Introduction 
 1. Roles and Responsibilities: 
 The Hostel Affairs Secretary shall: 
 a.  Look after the issues regarding the general maintenance of the hostels; 
 b.  Be the ex-officio student head of the Mess Monitoring and Controlling 
 Committee(MMCC), Committee for Monitoring of General Facilities of Students 
 (CMGFS), and Sustainability Committee(SusCom); 
 c.  Be responsible for monitoring the process of allocation of messes to the students; 
 d.  Be the principal representative of the Student Body to the Hostel Management in all matters, 
 including, but not restricted to, the procurement of equipment and supplies for hostels and 
 messes; 
 e.  Represent the students in all tendering processes for Mess facilities and CMGFS facilities 
 inside the Institute; 
 f.  Be one of the student members of any committee which may be formed for providing Hostel 
 amenities to the students; 
 g.  Be a member of the Hostel Disciplinary Committee (HDC); 
 h.  Be a permanent invitee to any of the Legislative Committees monitoring the hostel and 
 mess affairs of the students. 
 i.  Be accountable for providing the vendors for cycle mela during the 1st week of the 
 Academic year 
 2. Resignation Procedure of HAS: 
 a.  Resignation  procedure  of  HAS:  Any  member  of  the  Executive  Council  who  wishes  to  resign 
 his/her  Position  of  Responsibility  may  do  so  by  writing  a  resignation  letter  to  the  Speaker  and 
 the  Chairman,  Board  of  Students,  detailing  reasons  behind  the  same  along  with  the  password  to 
 their  official  email  id.  A  member  of  the  Executive  Council  shall  have  resigned  upon  official 
 communication of the same to the Student Legislative Council by the Speaker. 
 b.  Post-resignation  Procedure:  In  case  a  member  of  the  Executive  Council  resigns  with  more 
 than  three  months  available  for  the  scheduled  General  Elections,  the  Student  Election 
 Commission  shall  conduct  an  election  for  that  particular  Position  of  Responsibility 
 immediately,  with  the  student  so  elected  continuing  to  hold  office  till  the  end  of  the  academic 
 year.  In  other  cases,  the  Dean  (Students)  shall  give  additional  charge  of  the  vacated  Position  of 
 Responsibility to a member of the Executive Council. 
 3. Statement of Expenditure: 
 The  Executive  Council  and  Student  Government  Organisations  shall  present  a  financial  statement 
 detailing  the  expenditure  of  the  approved  budget  at  least  once  a  semester  to  the  Student  Legislative 
 Council  in  addition  to  a  report  about  their  financial  statement  to  the  corresponding  Standing 
 Committee  once  in  two  months.  Failure  to  do  the  same  may  lead  to  the  initiation  of  contempt 
 proceedings  against  the  secretary/body  and  bring  them/it  to  the  Student  Legislative  Council  for  further 
 action.

 Section IV: Removal Procedure 
 Removal Procedure: 
 Removal from the team shall be for two broad reasons: 
 1. Unsatisfactory performance 
 2. Disciplinary Action 
 Unsatisfactory Performance 
 If a selected team member is found performing below expectations, they can be 
 removed from the team following due procedure. An initial warning shall be given, with 
 a notice period of two weeks. If the performance does not improve in two weeks, the 
 members higher in the hierarchy (if any), in consultation with the respective chairman 
 can choose to remove the member from the team. A report on the same shall be 
 submitted to the Chairman of the respective committee and shall be made available to 
 SLC. 
 Disciplinary Action 
 In case any complaint is received against any selected member of the team accusing 
 them of disciplinary violations, this shall be taken seriously and can serve as grounds 
 for termination from the team. A committee shall be constituted by the Hostel Affairs 
 Secretary within 48 hours of receipt of the complaint. The committee shall investigate 
 the complaint thoroughly, the report of which will be made available to the SLC and 
 submitted to the Chairman of the respective committee along with a recommendation to 
 remove the said person from the team and prior work will not be credited if found 
 guilty. 
 Further, being found guilty and facing disciplinary action in any of the disciplinary 
 committees or statutory bodies such as the Committee for Complaints Against Sexual 
 Harassment (CCASH)can be deemed legitimate grounds for removal of a person from a 
 team, upon the discretion of the Hostel Affairs Secretary. Any action taken in this regard 
 must be communicated to the Chairman of the respective Committee and also 
 documented internally within the team.
