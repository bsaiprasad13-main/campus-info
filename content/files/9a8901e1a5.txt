History and Background 
The   Inter   IIT   Sports   Meet   is   organized   annually   among   all   the   IITs   where top athletes from 
the country’s best technical institutions are gathered at one place. It is organized annually among all the 
IITs by rotation/mutual consent at minimum two or more IITs.  
 
I. AIMS AND OBJECTIVES 
 
1. The primary objective of Inter IIT Sports Meet shall be to organize sports competitions in 
selected Olympics recognized events. This meet aims encourage sportsmanship, to promote and 
create a better harmony, understanding and friendly interaction among the students of the IITs, 
as well as to foster a close association among them. 
 
2. To make the students aware of the importance of sports and games in the modern era. 
 
3. To help students perform skillfully and effectively not only in sports, games and exercises but also 
in all situations. 
 
4. To raise the standard of sports in IITs and to work for the development of character and values 
through sports among the students of the IITs. 
 
5. To discuss matters related to the organization of sports and other such activities that support the 
aims and objectives of the Inter IIT Sports Meet, as mentioned above. 
 
6. The Sports Meet shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions  mentioned in 
succeeding paragraphs, which may be revised from time to time . 
 
 
II. INTER IIT SPORTS BOARD 
 
• The Inter IIT Sports Board shall be constituted for the purpose of effective supervision of 
the Inter IIT Sports Meet. The headquarters of the Sports Board will be located at all the hosting 
IITs. The Sports Board is constituted with the following members: 
 
1. The Directors of the hosting IITs will be the Chairperson of the Inter IIT Sports Board. 
 
2. The Deans, Students’ Affairs of the hosting IITs will be the Chairpersons of the Inter IIT 
Sports Meet. 
 
3. Chairpersons/Faculty advisors of each hosting IITs will be the Conveners of the Inter IIT 
Sports Meet. 
 
4. One Physical Education staff of each hosting IITs will be the Organizing Secretaries of 
the Inter IIT Sports Meet. 
 
5. Chairpersons/Faculty advisors of participating IITs at each hosting IIT will be the 
members of the Inter IIT Sports Meet. 
 
6. One Physical Education staff of participating IITs at each hosting IIT will be the member 
of the Inter IIT Sports Meet. 
 
7. One st udent General Secretary, Sports/student nominee of participating IITs at  each 
hosting IITs will be the member of the Inter IIT Sports Meet.

• Chairperson of the Sports Board shall preside over all Sports Board’s meetings in the 
respective IITs. In his/her absence, the Chairperson of the Inter IIT Sports Meet or Convener 
of Inter IIT Sports Board shall occupy the chair and conduct the proceedings of the meetings. 
The minutes of all the meetings shall be recorded and circulated to the members of all IITs 
immediately after each meeting. 
 
• The quorum for conducting of the Sports Board meeting shall be a minimum of half or more of 
the total members of the Sports Board. All proceedings shall be decided by a majority of votes. 
In case of a tie, the Chairperson or his/her nominee shall make the final decision on the matter. 
 
• The Sports Board of the Inter IIT Sports Meet may co-opt any individual in an advisory 
capacity, to be a member on ad hoc basis, without any voting right. 
 
• The Sports Board shall meet at the at one of the hosting IITs, as soon as possible, after the 
academic session begins for the year in which the Inter IIT Sports Meet will be conducted. 
The Board shall have two more mandatory meetings at the host IITs, one on the day preceding 
the inauguration of the Sports Meet and the other before the closing ceremony of the Sports 
Meet. Additional meetings shall be organized as desired by the Board. 
 
• In pursuance of the responsibilities vested in the Inter IIT Sports Meet Board, it shall have the 
following specific powers in connection with the conduct of the Sports Meet. 
 
a) To co-ordinate the Sports Meet, finalize the dates and to draws fixtures/schedule of the 
Meet. 
 
b) Addition or deletion of any sports/events. 
 
 
c) To amend, modify, formulate or delete the rules of Inter IIT Sports Meet with majority 
of votes among the present board members. 
 
d) To discuss matters related to the organization of sports and to promote other such 
activities that may be conducive to the aims and objectives of the Inter IIT Sports Meet. 
 
e) All the c ompetition shall be conducted as per the federation rules for each games and 
sports implemented by Indian Federations, or any modification approved by the board, 
till the date of the board meeting in August of the respective year. 
 
f) Over and above these powers, the Inter IIT Sports Board shall be the final authority to 
decide on any matter(s) arising during the Sports Meet and its decisions shall be binding 
to all the IITs.

VI. SCHEDULE AND DRAWS 
 
 
i. Hosting IITs shall run all the events in a ccordance with the schedule i .e. date and ti me as 
approved by the Sports Board. 
 
ii. The entries shall be sent to organizing secretaries by the participating IITs well in advance so 
that when the Sports Board meets during its first meeting in August, the Sports Board is able 
to arrange the entire program of the Sports Meet. Seeding will be awarded to the first four/two 
position holders of the respective game in the previous meet. Only two seeding will be given, 
if ten or less teams are participating in the respective game. Four teams will be given seeding, 
if more than ten teams are participating in the respective game. The remaining teams shall be 
considered by drawing lots for each game during the first meeting of sports board. 
 
iii. The schedule of the matches will be in accordance with the model fixture provided in the 
attached annexure. 
 
 
iv. If one team withdraws its entry and if, it was in the pool of 4 teams, no redraw of fixtures will 
be done. Similarly, if one team withdraws in a game having only two pools, no redraw of fixtures 
will be done. 
 
 
v. In all other cases of withdrawal of  one or more teams, redrawing  will be done and the 
previous fixture will be considered as null and void. 
 
 
vi. The team withdraw from fixture will be banned for next year in the respective game. 
 
 
 
vii. Organizing committee has the right to alter the schedule of the program under unusual 
circumstances.

X. OFFICIALS FOR THE MEET 
 
i. The organizing IITs shall be responsible for selecting competent umpires/referees whose 
decisions on all points of fact shall be final and binding to all participants. 
 
ii. The panel of umpires/referees shall be drawn by the host IITs well in advance. 
 
iii. Only qualified Association/Federation referees and umpires shall be appointed to supervise the 
Inter IIT Sports Meet. 
 
iv. In case of non-availability of Association/Federation referees, the organizing secretaries shall 
have the power to appoint suitable officials. 
 
v. In case of athletics, as far as possible, all the officials of the Inter IIT Athletic Meet should be FTO 
/ AFI qualified officials. If that is not possible, at least chief referees for throws, jumps and track 
events shall invariably be FTO/ AFI qualified. 
 
XI. CONDUCT/PROTEST 
 
i. A team/competitor shall be permitted a grace of 15 minutes from the schedule timings of the event 
and then a walk-over shall be awarded unless genuine reason(s), beyond control, are presented 
and accepted by the Board. 
 
ii. For all games and sports, the decision of the referees and the umpires will be final and binding 
to all participants. 
 
iii. No protest shall be entertained. Therefore, any team leaving the field of play as a mark of protest 
and conceding a walk-over shall be deemed to have lost the fixture and will be eliminated 
from rest of the event. Only the remaining teams will be considered as participating in that 
event. 
 
iv. Protest against the decision of an umpire/referee on a  point of rule and any other protest in 
connection with the concerned match/event shall be given in writing addressed to the Jury of 
appeal along with a protest fee of Rs.500. 
v. The written protest must reach the Organizing Secretary of the host institute within an hour of 
the conclusion of the concerned game duly signed by the competent authority of that 
particular IIT. 
 
vi. In case of a n event being conducted outside the IIT premises, the protest in writing shall be 
handed over to the Organizing Committee within an hour of arrival in the hosting IIT.

XVI. BADMINTON 
 
i. The competition shall be conducted under the Badminton World Federation (BWF) rules as 
adopted from time to time by the Badminton Association of India, unless otherwise modified. 
 
ii. The tournament for men and women section will be conducted on the lines of Inter State 
Badminton Championship. 
 
iii. Ties for men and women will be separately decided according to the pattern given below. 
 
a. The tie for men shall be decided by the combined result of 3 singles and 2 doubles. 
 
b. The tie for women by the results of 2 singles and one double. 
 
c. The result of a tie, ho wever, can be declared when e ither of t he competing team wins 3 
matches in men’s  section and 2 matches in the women’s section. 
 
iv. Matches will be played in the best of three sets (each set of 21 points) format. The order of events 
shall be as follows: 
 
a. MEN: First Single/Second Single/First Double/Third Single/Second Double 
 
b. WOMEN: First Single/Double/ Second Single 
 
v. Number of players: 
 
a. Men: The number of players representing any IIT in a team can be a maximum of 5 
members. 
 
b. Women: The number of players representing any IIT in a team can be a maximum of 3 
members. 
 
vi. Tie will be resolved by successively applying the following criteria: 
 
a. Individual matches won – (minus) individual matches lost. 
 
b. Games won by the team – (minus) games lost by the team. 
 
c. Points for – (minus) Points against. 
 
vii. The tally for all the matches played in the league fixture will be considered.

XVII. BASKETBALL 
 
i. The tournament will be conducted according to the International Basketball Federation Rules 
(FIBA) as adopted from time to time by the Indian Basketball Federation, unless otherwise 
modified. 
 
In Men section, the number of players representing any IIT shall not be more than 12. 
 
 
ii. In Women section, the number of players representing any IIT shall not be more than 12. 
 
 
iii. During matches if the score is the same after the end of the scheduled time, Basketball 
Federation Rules will be applied, i.e. the game shall continue with as many extra periods of 5 
minutes as is necessary to break the tie. 
 
 
iv. On completion of the league matches, if two or more teams are on same points, then the tie 
shall be resolved as follows: 
 
a. If two teams are tied head-to-head, a win among the teams will be considered to 
break the tie. 
 
 
b. If more than two teams are equal in placing, a second classification will be established, 
taking into account only the results of the games between the teams that have tied. 
 
 
c. If there are still teams tied alter the second classification, the basket average score will 
be used to determine the placing, taking into account only the results of the games 
between the teams that tied. 
 
 
d. If there are still teams tied, the placing will be determined using the basket 
average score from the results of all their games played in the group. 
 
 
 
XVIII. CHESS 
 
i. As Chess is a common event for both men and women, only one trophy will be awarded. 
 
ii. The time control will be 90 minutes per player for the whole game with an increment of 30 
seconds per move from move number one. 
 
iii. Writing the score sheet is mandatory. 
 
iv. Latest FIDE laws of chess shall be followed. 
 
v. The arbiter’s decision shall be final and binding. 
 
vi. The issue regarding use of mobile phones and other electronic devices being allowed in the 
playing hall shall be discussed in the players meeting with the arbiter along with other 
minor tournament rules. 
 
vii. Swiss league system will be followed with the number of rounds decided on the basis of the 
number of participating teams. The pairing of each round will be decided by the software.

BEST LIFTER 
 
 
Sinclair coefficients formula: 
The Sinclair coefficients, derived statistically, are adjusted each Olympic year and are based on 
the Total World Records in the various bodyweight categories as of the previous several years. 
 
ACTUAL TOTAL x SINCLAIR COEFFICIENT = SINCLAIR TOTAL 
 
 
The Sinclair Total is calculated by taking an athlete’s total (combined max snatch and clean & 
jerk) and multiplying that number by the Sinclair Coefficient.  
 
Example: Who is the strongest? 
 
If 50kg man totals 178kg, the 100kg man totals 303kg and the 130kg man totaling 323kg? The math 
looks like: 
50kg male has a Sinclair coefficient of 1.713308.

--- page 27 ---
P a g e  27 | 29 
 
178kg (total) x 1.713308 = 304.969 (Sinclair Total) 
 
 
The 100kg male’s coefficient is 1.112602. 
303kg (total) x 1.112602 = 337.118 
 
The 130kg’s coefficient is 1.030222. 
323kg (total) x 1.030222 = 332.762 
 
The strongest man by this definition is the 100kg man. 
 
 
Sinclair scores more fairly c omparing lif ters of  different body w eights. The score is obtained  by 
multiplying the lifter's Snatch and Clean & Jerk total by a coefficient which is based on body weight. 
The total lifts of the gold medal winners in every weight class at each Olympic Games are used to 
determine the formula that will be used for calculating Sinclair coefficients. Thus, Sinclair coefficients 
and scores are only valid for the four-year period commencing on 01 January of the year immediately 
following an Olympic Games.
