*ACR Office Scholarships
S.no | Code | Scholarship Name | Criteria
1 | AMCM | Alumni Merit-cum-Means Scholarship | (i) Parental income between Rs. 1 lakh and Rs. 5 lakh per annum
(ii) B. Tech. / Dual Degree / B S students are eligible.
(iii) Up to four (4) years of tuition assistance will be covered.
(iv) Should not be a recipient of any other scholarship.
(v) Each student will receive support of Rs. 66,666 per year
(vi) Beneficiaries need to maintain the minimum CGPA of 6.5 to continue eligibility for support.
2 | ESS | Extended Support Scheme Endowment | (i) Parental income between Rs. 5 lakh and Rs. 8 lakhs per annum.
(ii) Degree: B. Tech/Dual Degree/BS
(iii) Up to four (4) years of tuition assistance will be covered.
(iv) Should not be a recipient of any other scholarship.
(vi) Beneficiaries need to maintain the minimum CGPA of 6.5 to continue eligibility for support.
(vii) Each student will receive support of Rs. 2,00,000 per year
3 | SLE 1 | Support for Living Expenses - PwD |  (i) Only students belonging to the PWD category are eligible to receive this scholarship with the below Rs. 8 lakhs income per annum
 (ii) Degree: B.Tech/B.S./Dual Degree are only eligible
 (iii) Should not be a recipient of any other scholarship. 
 (iv) 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th year students are eligible for this Scholarship. 
 (v) Subject to maintaining of a minimum CGPA of 5 for renewal.
 (vi) Submission of valid disability certificate is mandatory to receive this schoalrship
(vii) Each student will receive living support of Rs. 1,00,000 per year
4 | SLE 2 | Support for Living Expenses - SC/ST |  (i) The student’s annual parental income should be up to Rs. 8 lakh per annum 
 (ii) Degree: B.Tech/B.S./Dual Degree are only eligible
 (iii) The scholarship renewal is subject to maintaining a minimum CGPA of 5 and submission of a valid annual income certificate every year. 
 (iv)1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th year students are eligible for this Scholarship. 
 (v) Should not be a recipient of any other scholarship
 (vi) Students from SC/ST category are only eligible
 (vii) Each student will receive living support of Rs. 1,00,000 per year
5 | SLE 3 | Support for Living Expenses - Below 1L |   (i) Parental income below Rs. 1 lakh per annum. 
  (ii) Degree:B.Tech/B.S./Dual Degree are only eligible
  (iii) Should not be a recipient of any other scholarship. 
  (iv) 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th year students are eligible for this Scholarship. 
  (v) Subject to maintaining of a minimum CGPA of 5 and submission of a valid Annual income certificate every year.
 (vi) Each student will receive living support of Rs. 1,00,000 per year
