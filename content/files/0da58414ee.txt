Roles and Responsibilities
The Academic Affairs Secretary (AAS) shall:
● Represent the views of the students from taught programs on academic matters to the Board of AcademicCourses and the Senate. These include, but are not limited to the academic program, the curriculum, teacherand course feedback, academic clubs and activities, and undergraduate and postgraduate research.
● Coordinate between the Executives at the department level regarding issues that concern more than onedepartment.
● Coordinate the functioning of all the centralised facilities of academic nature, such as the Library, P. G. SenapathyComputer Centre, Placement Office, etc. along with the Research Affairs Secretary in a manner that addressesthe needs of the General Student Body.
● The Academic Affairs Secretary, along with the Research Affairs Secretary, shall be responsible for the smoothfunctioning of these facilities including, but not restricted to, the number, variety and quality of books andjournals available to the General Student Body.
● Be an ex-officio member of any committee, which may be set up by the Administration for matters related to theacademic affairs for taught programs.
● Be a member of the Sub-Committee for Discipline and Welfare of Students and regularly update the StudentLegislative Council and the General Student Body regarding the decisions taken by the Committee.
● Coordinate with the International and Alumni Relations Secretary on promoting research collaborations betweenIIT Madras and other premier Institutes/Universities in India and around the world.
● Be conversant with all the rules of academic research at the Institute.
● The following teams will be headed by the Academic Affairs Secretary:
➔ Placement & Internship Cell concerning the students of all the taught programs
➔ Career Development Cell

Placement & Internship Cell
The Placement and Internship Cell shall facilitate industrial as well as academic opportunities via established processessuch as the Institute Placement Process & Institute Internship Process and other de-centralised processes.
The cell shall be led by the Placement and Internship Governing Council, comprising of 5 Heads of the Placement &Internship Cell. They are:
1. Academic Affairs Secretary (AAS)2. Research Affairs Secretary (RAS)3. Placement Head (for all taught programs)4. Placement Head (for all research programs)5. Internship Head (for all taught programs)
The team under the Placement and Internship Governing Council can be broadly divided into:
1. Verticals:a. These are the intra-department teams which are three-tieredb. The department teams will be separate for both the UG and PG programs, with representation for alldepartments in the UG and PG teams.c. The internship process will be jointly run by the same team for all the teams relevant to taughtprograms.2. Horizontals:a. These are the inter-department teams which are two-tieredb. These teams will handle all the things that aren’t under the purview of the department teams.
Kindly refer to the Rulebook for the Placement & Internship Cell for more details.

Career Development Cell
The primary duty of the Career Development Cell is to do all career development activities at a department level(through the Department Academic Mentorship program) and Institute level in coordination with CDC – R. In addition tothis, the CDC will look after the career-based clubs and the Student Guidance UG Team under the Placement &Internship Cell.
Team Structure under the AAS
CDC Heads
The CDC Heads directly report to the AAS and are appointed through a written application and an interview process. Amaximum of 3 CDC Heads may be selected. The interview panel shall contain the AAS and outgoing CDC Heads. Advisor(CDC) may choose to have an additional interview process followed by the above-mentioned one. The CDC Heads are thede-facto Cores of the UG Student Guidance Team in the Placement & Internship Cell.
Deputy Heads and Managers
The CDC Heads shall select Deputy Heads and managers, in consultation with the AAS. The selection process is through awritten application and an interview. The interview panel shall contain the CDC Heads; the AAS may choose to join. TheCDC Deputy Heads and managers are the de-facto Deputy Heads and managers of the UG Student Guidance Team in thePlacement & Internship Cell.
Duties and Responsibilities
CDC is divided into 3 verticals. Each vertical would be assigned Deputy heads who would have the responsibility ofheading their respective verticals and managers who would be responsible for the operations of each vertical namely:
1. Placement and internship vertical: This vertical would have responsibilities of organising sessions to aid studentswith their placement and internship preparation, vendor outreach which can aid students in preparation, aidstudents to prepare in growing in their upcoming corporate careers and other activities which the appointedheads would see fit in counsel with the CDC heads.2. Future Prep vertical: This vertical would be responsible for helping students in their journey to find paths theycan take for their future by organising sessions and activities in the domain of higher studies, Competitive examsand Research, to handle niche career sessions, facilitate activities for students in tandem with the Universityrelations team and workshops and other activities which the appointed heads would see fit in counsel with theCDC heads.3. Mentorship vertical: The Mentorship team will handle the non-standard sessions and the Department AcademicMentorship program (DAMP) facilitating career development and academic activities at the department level.More detailed description of this vertical is mentioned on page 7. Other activities which the appointed headswould see fit in counsel with the CDC heads.

Removal Procedure
Removal from the team shall be for the following reason:
Unsatisfactory Performance
If a selected team member is found performing way below the expectations, he/she can be removed from the CDC teamfollowing due procedure. An initial warning shall be given, with a notice period of two weeks. If the performance doesnot improve in two weeks, the members higher in the hierarchy (if any), in consultation with AAS and Advisor (CDC), canchoose to remove the member from the team. A report on the same shall be submitted to the Advisor (CDC) and shall bemade available to SLC.

Department Academic Mentorship Program
The Department Academic Mentorship Program (DAMP), managed by mentorship vertical - CDC, facilitates academicsand career mentorship at the department level. The primary target for the program is to guide second year and thirdyear dual degree students.
Team structure under CDC heads and AAS
Department Heads
1.Department heads would be appointed in consultation with the respective HoD and outgoing Department Head.2. The respective Department Legislators (Academic) can choose to be the ex-officio Department Student Heads ofDAMP.
Department Coordinator
The Department Heads from each department shall select upto 3 coordinators, exact number decided basis departmentstrength and stream diversity, in consultation with the CDC heads. The selection process is through a written applicationand an interview. The interview panel shall contain the incoming Department Heads; the CDC heads may choose to join.
Academic Advisor
The Department Heads from each department shall select upto 10 Academic Advisors, exact number decided basisdepartment strength and stream diversity, in consultation with the CDC heads and their selection process is donethrough a written application. The application procedure will be intimated to the student community in a call forapplications. The interview panel shall contain the incoming Department Heads; the CDC heads may choose to join.
Duties and Responsibilities
Department Head and Coordinator
All academic and career mentorship activities at the department level and coordinate with council for institute levelactivities. A calendar for academic and career mentorship activities at a department level has to be created by the teamwhich will then be executed at the department level.
Academic Advisor
The Advisor will be educating students by providing guidance on topics including, but not limited to:
● The scope of the department and potential career opportunities
● The different specialisations offered by the program
● The kind of research opportunities the department offers
● Balancing academics and extracurricular activities
● Going about selecting courses and building a profile

Removal Procedure
One can be disqualified from the team only upon consulting the CDC faculty Advisor and submitting a request fordisqualification along with a report.

If theperformance does not improve in two weeks CDC heads in consultation with AAS and the CDC faculty Advisor can chooseto remove the member from the team. Finance Club
The primary duty of the Finance Club is to facilitate a platform for finance enthusiasts in the institute to engage and helpto develop a culture for the same among the interested members of the GSB
Team Structure under the AAS & CDC Heads
Finance Club Heads
The Finance Club Heads directly report to the AAS and the CDC Heads and are appointed through a written applicationand an interview process. The interview panel shall contain the AAS and outgoing Finance Club Heads. Advisor (CDC) maychoose to have an additional interview process followed by the above-mentioned one.
Coordinator, Magazine Chief Editor
The Finance Club Heads shall select Coordinators and Magazine Chief Editor, in consultation with the AAS. The selectionprocess is through a written application and an interview. The interview panel shall contain the Finance Club Heads; theAAS may choose to join.
Deputy Coordinators, Project Members, Magazine Correspondents
The Finance Club Heads shall select Deputy Coordinators, Project Members and Magazine Correspondents, inconsultation with the AAS. The selection process is through a written application and an interview. The interview panelshall contain the Finance Club Heads and Coordinator / Magazine Chief Editor, based on the team structure; the AAS maychoose to join.
Quant & Investment Projects Vertical
The team will have 2 Project Directors, 5-6 Permanent Members and rolling members selected through an engagementprogramme. Projects with a duration greater than 6 months will be taken up by QIP, and projects with a duration lessthan 6 months will be handled by the 3 main verticals. In a situation where either wants to take up a project from theothers’ side, there will have to be a proper discussion prior to the project being conducted as a collaboration. Here,Finclub(3 Main Verticals) and QIP will have the final say in their respective duration guidelines based on jurisdiction.

Behavioural Insights Club
The club would focus on serving as a platform for students and key industry stakeholders in the field of BehaviouralScience to get together and tackle real-world issues while upskilling themselves in the process. BInC will play a principalrole in introducing and shaping an unconventional career path in the academic ecosystem of IIT Madras.
Our Vision for this club can be briefly encapsulated with the following;
“Leverage understanding of Engineering, Management, and Psychology to gain interdisciplinary insights to addressreal-world problems.”
Team Structure under the AAS & CDC Heads
BInC Head
The BInC Heads directly report to the AAS and the CDC Heads and are appointed through a written application and aninterview process. The interview panel shall contain the AAS and outgoing Head along with strat. Advisor (CDC) maychoose to have an additional interview process followed by the above-mentioned one.
Strat
The strat are appointed through an interview process. The interview panel shall contain outgoing Head along withoutgoing strat; the AAS may choose to join.
Leads
The BInC Head shall select Leads, in consultation with the AAS. The selection process is through a written application andan interview. The interview panel shall contain the BInC Head and strat; the AAS may choose to join.
Project Member
The BInC Head shall select Project Members, in consultation with the AAS. The selection process is through a writtenapplication and an interview. The interview panel shall contain the BInC Head and the respective project lead; the AASmay choose to join.
Duties and Responsibilities
The set of activities that can be taken up by the Behavioural Insights Club include (but are not limited to):
● Various Social Experiments: Organise insti-wide sessions, where GSB will be invited to participate and leave witha better idea of nudges and behavioural sciences
