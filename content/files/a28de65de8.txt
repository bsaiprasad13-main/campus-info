Roles and Responsibilities: 
The Research Affairs Secretary shall: 
 
A. Represent the views of the students from research programs on matters related to academic research 
to the Board of Academic Research and the Senate. These include, but are not limited to the research 
programme, the curriculum, teacher, guide and course feedback, and postgraduate research; 
 
B. Coordinate between the Executives at the depar tment level regarding issues which concern more  
than one department; 
 
C. Coordinate the functioning of all the centralized facilities of academic nature, such as the Library, the 
P. G. Senapathy Computer Centre, the Placement Office etc. along with the Academ ic Affairs  
Secretary in a manner that addresses the needs of the General Student Body. The Research Affairs  
Secretary, along with the Academic Affairs Secretary, shall be responsible for the smooth functioning 
of these facilities including, but not restricted to, the number, variety and quality of books and  
journals available to the General Student Body; 
 
D. Be an ex -officio member of any committee which may be set up by the Administration for matters  
related to the affairs of academic research of the students; 
 
E. Be a member of the Academic Disciplinary Committee and regularly update the Student Legislative  
Council and the General Student Body regarding the decisions taken by the Committee; 
 
F. Be a member of the Ethics Committee and regularly update the Student Legislative Council and the  
General Student Body regarding the decisions taken by the Committee for issues regarding unethical 
publishing/plagiarism etc. 
 
G. Coordinate with the International and Alumni Relations Secretary on promoting research 
collaborations between IIT Madras and other premier Institutes/Universities in India and around the 
world; 
 
H. Be conversant with all the rules of academic research of the Institute.

2. Placement & Internship Cell: 
 
The Placement and Internship Cell shall facilitate industrial as well as academic opportunities via 
established processes such as the Institute Placement Process & Institute Internship Process and other de-
centralised processes. 
RAS during the 2023-2024 academic year proposed a new placement division that deals with the academics 
related opportunities is planned to be created under the existing placement cell. This idea arrived from the 
recent placement statistics indicating the scholars are equally motivated to take the academic positions like  
pdf positions, Faculty positions,, Teaching positions , Foreign exchange programs, and Research associate 
at prestigious institutes in India.  The academic placement head works under the RAS and need to report 
to the RAS. The motive of this division is to provide the academic opportunities to the scholars. The head is 
responsible to identify the vacancies related to the faculty positions at various institutes and make them 
available to the scholars either by on -campus or off -campus means. This initiative helps scholars land in 
academic roles at premier institutes.

2. Career Development Cell – Research (CDC-R) Team: 
The CDC-R is an umbrella team for conceptualizing, undertaking and implementing all activities focused 
towards the personal and professional development of the scholars of the Institute. It handles all 
activities aimed towards enriching, empowering and enhancing the information, opportunities and 
facilities available to a scholar on campus. This in turn helps them decide and advance in their desired 
career choice with the right set of tools and outlook. The set of activities  which can be taken up  by 
CDC-R includes, but not limited to: 
 
 
 
● Placement Preparation 
● Scholars Career Sessions 
● Mentorship with Industries 
● Entrepreneurship Workshops 
● Skills Enhancement Workshops 
● Scholars Website (scholars.iitm.ac.in) 
● Any and all initiatives aimed at improving the career prospects of the scholars 
 
Head - CDC-R: 
The CDC-R Head directly reports to the Research Affairs Secretary, and is appointed through a written 
application and an interview process. The interview panel shall contain the outgoing CDC -R Head and 
Research Affairs Secretary, the incoming Research Affairs Secretary and approved by the Advisor (CDC-
R) . In case the position is unfilled (no applicant or all applicants are rejected by the panel), the Research 
Affairs Secretary shall occupy this position by default.

3. Research Scholars Day (RSD) Team: 
The Research Scholars Day (RSD) team shall be responsible for conducting the annual research festival 
of IIT Madras, Research Scholars Day . The RSD would be the flagship festival of the Institute to  
celebrate the spirit of Research on the Campus and provide opportunities for the scholars to showcase 
their talents and interact with the distinguished industry and academic personalities including the 
alumni as well. 
 
The RSD Team shall be constituted for conducting this festival  and other Scholar interaction events, 
and the CDC-R team and all other teams shall assist them with all possible help they would be needed. 
A few verticals shall be specific to the RSD  team and recruitment shall be done for them separately at 
the appropriate t ime. Each of the verticals  would be headed by 1 or 2 Cores and they shall have 
coordinators assisting them in executing the activities.

6. Problem-Solving Cell – Research: 
Under the RAS, the problem-solving cell is a body responsible for solving problems faced by scholars at 
an institute and residential level. With an idea to help scholars solve their issues, a new Problem-solving 
cell has been proposed by the RAS during the 2023 -2024 academic year. The motivation to help 
scholars has arrived from the recent unfortunate events happened inside the campus. The pr ime 
motive of this unit is to identify the common problems faced by scholars through surveys and arrive at 
the solutions to resolve the issues. The head of the problem -solving cell should directly report to the 
RAS. The problem -solving cell deals with all kinds of issues faced by scholars such as work -place 
harassment, academic/administration related issues, guide -student relationships, mental health etc. 
Scholars facing mental health issues can approach the head of the problem-solving cell. With the help 
of wellness centre and MiTr,  Sathi  the student will be given a proper counselling in order to prevent 
the scholar taking any sensitive actions. During the entire process, the identity of the scholar will be 
protected and anonymity is maintained. The structure of the problem-solving cell is created with 4 
cores responsible in carrying out various activities with the help of the coordinators. The cores will have 
regular meetings with the head to discuss the progress and jointly come up with common ideas to 
resolve the scholar’s issues. Important decisions that can have a potential impact on the administrative 
activities should be discussed with the RAS and the RAS need to make a final decision preserving the 
common interests of the scholars 
 
Head - Problem-Solving Cell – Research: 
The Problem solving cell - Research Head directly reports to the Research Affairs Secretary, and is 
appointed through a  written application and an interview process. The interview panel shall contain 
the outgoing past PSC-R Head and Research Affairs Secretary, the incoming Research Affairs Secretary 
and approved by the Advisor (PSC-R) . In case the position is unfilled (no applicant or all applicants are 
rejected by the panel), the Research Affairs Secretary shall occupy this position by default.

Due to the removal of the Councilor position from the Constitution in 2020, RAC is suspended. The

Resignation Procedure 
 
Resignation of the Research Affairs Secretary: 
Any member of the Executive Council who wishes to resign his/her Position of Responsibility may do so by 
writing a resignation letter to the Speaker and the Chairman, Board of Students, detailing reasons behind  
the same along with the password to their officia l email -id. The Research Affairs Secretary shall have  
resigned upon official communication of the same to the Student Legislative Council by the Speaker. 
 
Post-resignation Procedure: 
In case a member of the Executive Council resigns with more than three months available for the scheduled 
General Elections, the Student Election Commission shall conduct an election for that particular Position of 
Responsibility immediately, with the student so elected continuing to hold office till the end of the academic 
year. In other cases, the Dean (Students) shall give additional charge of the vacated Position  of 
Responsibility to a member of the Executive Council. 
 
Statement of Expenditure 
The Executive Council and Student Government Organizations shall present a financial statement detailing 
the expenditure of the approved budget at least once a semester to the Student Legislative Council in  
addition to a report about their financial statement to the corresponding Standing Committee once in two 
months. Failure to do the same may lead to the initiation of contempt proceedings against the 
secretary/body and bring them/it to Student Legislative Council for further action.
